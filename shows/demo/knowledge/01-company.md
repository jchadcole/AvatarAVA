About Genesys:
- Genesys describes itself as "the agentic AI CX platform for the enterprise." Its AI is purpose-built for customer experience, connecting customer intent to people, systems and workflows.
- Tagline on the home page: "Stop automating interactions. Start orchestrating experiences."
- It calls itself an "AI-first company with a human center," focused on "AI for the experience economy."
- Founded in 1990, with more than 25 years of history in customer experience.
- More than 8,000 customer organizations in over 100 countries, across six continents.
- More than 6,000 employees.
- Headquarters: Menlo Park, California.
- Lead product: Genesys Cloud (Genesys Cloud CX).
- Beyond software, Genesys offers professional services (onboarding, training and strategic consulting) and a global partner network of technology partners and system integrators.
- The site also has pages on Responsible AI, a Trust Center for security, and sustainability.
