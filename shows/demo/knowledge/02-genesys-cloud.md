Genesys Cloud:
- An agentic AI customer experience platform that delivers personalized customer and employee experiences across every channel, as one platform for the whole customer organization.
- On every interaction it runs a continuous loop: perceive, reason, act, optimize and govern.
- Four main areas:
  - AI automation: a workbench to build, test and govern AI agents. Virtual agents give 24/7 service in any language, grounded in approved knowledge, across voice, text, web, social and WhatsApp, inbound and outbound.
  - Customer journey management: unifies data, memory and real-time context, routes each customer to the right person, AI agent or flow with context kept, and connects front- and back-office teams to finish multi-step work.
  - Employee augmentation: one AI-powered workspace with copilots, context and knowledge, plus real-time performance insights for coaching, learning, goals and recognition. It can be embedded in CRM workflows.
  - Analytics and optimization: monitors both human and AI conversations for quality, forecasts volume and plans capacity across human and AI agents, and offers custom dashboards for KPIs, root-cause analysis and ROI.
- Channels: voice, email, web chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram and X direct messages, Apple Messages for Business, and open messaging for custom channels. Context carries across channels so customers don't have to repeat themselves.
- Integrations: prebuilt integrations with Salesforce and ServiceNow, plus the AppFoundry marketplace and open APIs. The site says Genesys has "the deepest partner ecosystem in CX."
- Platform: built natively on a single codebase, API-first and open, with developer tools.
- Scale, as stated for fiscal year 2026: 29 billion real-time conversations a year, 2 million Genesys Cloud users, and more than 7 billion conversations a quarter.
- Average time to go live for customers: 54 days. More than 30 compliance certifications.
- A free trial is available from genesys.com. Pricing is not given on this page; a booth teammate handles pricing.
- Built for AI strategists, contact center operations, service teams, and IT and developers.
