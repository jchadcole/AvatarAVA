Genesys Cloud AI:
- Combines conversational AI, generative AI and predictive AI in one platform, described as turnkey and out of the box.
- The AI Experience bundle combines bots, knowledge, predictive engagement, predictive routing and agent copilot.
- Genesys says its AI follows strict ethics guidelines, with built-in security, privacy, transparency and fairness.

Genesys Cloud AI Studio:
- A central hub, with AI Guides, for building, testing and scaling AI-powered experiences and orchestrating human and AI work.

Agentic Virtual Agent (AVA):
- An AI agent that goes beyond scripted bots: it reasons, plans and carries out multi-step tasks across systems, end to end, while keeping context across voice and digital channels.
- It handles off-script moments and missing data by re-planning, and hands off to a human, with context, only when needed.
- It can look up or update customer information, process transactions, troubleshoot, and run multi-step workflows.
- Powered by large action models that use approved tools and guardrails; every action is planned, validated and logged, so decisions are explainable and auditable.
- Built with no-code design tools in AI Studio, with analytics and full audit trails.
- Benefits Genesys lists: lower operating costs, better first-contact resolution, scalable support and more consistent experiences.

Copilots:
- Intelligent, proactive assistance for agents, supervisors and administrators, native to Genesys Cloud and limited to each user's permissions.
- Agent Copilot gives real-time help during customer conversations: knowledge surfaced automatically, next-best-action guidance, the right tool or form, and custom scripting.
- After a conversation, generative AI writes the summary and highlights key takeaways.
- Translates transcripts in more than 70 languages.
- Users can ask questions about metrics and trends in plain language instead of digging through dashboards.
- Supervisor Copilot summarizes interactions, translates, and generates insights so supervisors can focus on coaching.

Other AI capabilities:
- Predictive routing matches each customer with the best-suited agent while optimizing a chosen KPI.
- Predictive engagement reads website visitor behavior to understand intent and engage proactively.
- Knowledge delivers the right information to customers and agents when they need it and measures how well it works.
- Conversational intelligence analyzes conversations for sentiment, topic trends and agent empathy.
