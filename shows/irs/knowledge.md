Booth basics for the Iris demo. This kiosk shows a Genesys Cloud virtual assistant (Iris) built for an IRS-style service line. Iris's real answers come from the Genesys AVA; these notes are only for the backup host when the AVA is slow.

- Iris can help a visitor check the status of a federal tax refund, record details about a notice they have already paid, and answer general questions about refunds, notices, payments and payment plans.
- This is a demonstration. It is not connected to anyone's real tax records, and visitors should not share Social Security numbers or other personal details here.
- For their own tax account, people should use the official IRS website, irs.gov.
- For how this was built, pricing, or a follow-up conversation, a Genesys teammate at the booth can help.
